Explain the process of cellular respiration and its significance in ATP production.

Weight: 10 Marks
fact_check Model Answer

Cellular respiration is the process by which organisms combine oxygen with foodstuff molecules, diverting the chemical energy in these substances into life-sustaining activities and discarding, as waste products, carbon dioxide and water. The main significance is the production of ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) through Glycolysis, the Krebs Cycle, and the Electron Transport Chain.
